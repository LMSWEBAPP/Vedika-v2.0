Key Takeaways:
- **Core Concept**: Mitochondria act as microscopic power plants inside cells, transforming raw biochemical fuels into usable cellular energy.
- **Biochemical Reaction**: Cellular respiration combines glucose (from food) and oxygen (from breathing) to break down chemical bonds and release energy.
- **Energy Transfer Mechanism**: Released energy is captured by converting low-energy ADP into high-energy ATP, which functions like a rechargeable battery for the cell.
- **Physiological Application**: High-energy ATP molecules directly fuel critical life processes, including muscular movement, cardiovascular pumping, and neural brain activity.
