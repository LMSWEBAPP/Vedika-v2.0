Key Takeaways:
- **Core Concept**: Mitochondria function as the primary cellular power plants, converting stored chemical fuel into dynamic biological energy.
- **Transformation Mechanism**: Cellular respiration breaks down glucose in the presence of oxygen, extracting energy while releasing carbon dioxide and water as natural byproducts.
- **Energy Storage**: Energy is packaged into ATP (Adenosine Triphosphate), which acts as a molecular "rechargeable battery" that moves freely through the cell.
- **Physiological Application**: ATP directly powers vital biological functions, driving physical muscle movement, sustaining autonomous cardiac rhythms, and fueling complex cognitive processing in the brain.
